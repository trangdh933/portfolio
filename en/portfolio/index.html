AI Operations Marketing

AI Operations Marketing: running marketing with AI agents, every external output approved by a human

I design how AI agents work in marketing as an operating system: every stage has a responsible role, a checkpoint and a handoff file. Agents take on the repetitive work such as monthly planning, writing briefs and copy, posting, registering assets and building reports; the owner decides at the approval gates.

This describes how the system operates, not a promise of results.
